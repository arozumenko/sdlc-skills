# Interview guide — <role>

**Goal in one sentence:** *the single most valuable thing to walk out knowing.*

## In-room craft (reference, not a script)

*Fold these into the conversation; don't read them out.*

- **Ask about the past, not the future.** "Walk me through the last time you…" beats "Would you use…". Past behavior is data; predicted behavior is a wish.
- **Never pitch.** The moment you describe the solution, you stop learning. Keep the product out of the room until the pains are on the table.
- **Listen 80 / talk 20.** Silence pulls out the real answer. Count to three before filling it.
- **Five probing moves when something interesting surfaces:** (1) "Tell me more about that." (2) "Why?" — up to two or three times, laddering to the root. (3) "Can you give me a specific example?" (4) "What happened next / what did you do then?" (5) "How did that make you feel?"
