# T-9 — Slugs for article URLs

**Story.** As an editor I want every article title turned into a clean URL slug so links are readable and stable.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The slug is lowercase ASCII: accented letters lose their accents (`Crème brûlée` → `creme-brulee`).
2. Every run of characters other than `a-z` and `0-9` becomes a single `-`; there is no leading or trailing `-`.
3. The slug is **at most 64 characters**. A longer slug is cut at the last `-` before the limit, so no word is split and there is no trailing `-`.
4. An empty or all-punctuation title gives an empty slug.

Usage: `node src/cli.js "<title>"` prints the slug.
