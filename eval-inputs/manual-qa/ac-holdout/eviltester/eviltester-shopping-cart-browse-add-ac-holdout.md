## Feature: Shopping Cart — Browsing and Adding Products

As a shopper, I want to browse the product grid and add items to my cart, so that I can build up an order.

Acceptance Criteria:
- The product grid shows a set of product cards, each with an Add to Cart button and a View button, and a cart-count indicator that starts at zero for a fresh visit.
- Adding a single product to the cart increases the cart-count indicator by one.
- Adding several products, including adding the same product more than once, increases the cart-count indicator by one for each add action performed, regardless of repeats.
