## Feature: Drag and Drop Zones

As a user, I want to drag items onto drop zones and get clear, correct feedback about whether each drop is accepted, so that I understand which combinations are allowed.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Dragging the first item onto either drop zone is always accepted, showing a "Dropped!" confirmation.
- Dragging the second item onto the left drop zone is rejected, showing a "Get Off Me!" message, while dragging that same second item onto the right drop zone is accepted and shows "Dropped!".
