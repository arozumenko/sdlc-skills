## Feature: Shopping Cart — Pagination and Product Detail

As a shopper, I want to page through the full product catalog and view details for a specific product, so that I can explore everything on offer before buying.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Navigating to the next page of products shows a genuinely different set of products from the first page, not a repeat of it.
- Clicking View on a specific product opens a detail view for that exact product, matching what was shown for it in the grid.
