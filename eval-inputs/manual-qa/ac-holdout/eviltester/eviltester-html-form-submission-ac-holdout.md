## Feature: HTML Form Submission

As a user, I want to fill in the classic HTML form and submit it, so that my details are recorded and confirmed back to me.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Filling in the username, password, comments, a checkbox, a radio option, and a dropdown selection, then clicking the Submit button, shows a results page confirming my submission was received.
- The results page echoes back the values I entered (username, password, comments) and does not show any warning about the submission being invalid.
