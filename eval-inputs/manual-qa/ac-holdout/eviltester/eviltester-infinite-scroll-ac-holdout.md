## Feature: Infinite Scroll Content Loading

As a user, I want more content to load automatically as I scroll down, so that I don't need pagination controls to keep browsing.

Acceptance Criteria:
- The page loads with an initial set of content blocks visible.
- Scrolling to the bottom of the page loads and appends additional content blocks, increasing the total count.
- Scrolling to the bottom again loads further additional content, confirming this keeps working on repeated scrolls, not just once.
