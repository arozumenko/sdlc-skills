## Feature: Section Navigation Index Pages

As a visitor, I want each top-level section of the site to have its own index page listing what's inside it, so that I can quickly see what content is available before drilling in.

Acceptance Criteria:
- The Pages section's index page lists its sub-categories, including at least Basics, Input Elements, Forms, Files, and Auth.
- The Apps section's index page lists its standalone applications, including at least Triangle, Basic Shopping Cart, 7 Char Val, and Button Calculator.
- The Challenges section's index page lists both of its sub-categories: Locators and Synchronization.
