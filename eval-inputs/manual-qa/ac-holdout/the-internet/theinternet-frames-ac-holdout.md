# Acceptance criteria — theinternet-frames-ac-holdout

## Feature: Frame-Based Content

As a user, I want pages built from frames and iframes to display their
content correctly, so that content embedded this way is just as readable
as content on a normal page.

Acceptance Criteria:
- On the nested frames page, each of the four frame areas (left, middle,
  right, and a separate bottom area) shows its own distinct, correctly
  labeled content.
- On the iframe page, the embedded editor component loads inside its frame
  with visible content, and that content can be read.
