# Acceptance criteria — saucedemo-problem-user-distinct-ac-holdout

## Feature: Product photos are distinct per product

As a shopper, I want each product to show its own accurate photo, so that
I can tell products apart visually before buying.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Different products on the inventory page show different, distinct
  images — e.g. the Sauce Labs Backpack and Sauce Labs Bike Light product
  photos should not be the same image as each other.

Example: logging in with the account `problem_user` / `secret_sauce` and
comparing the Backpack and Bike Light product images should follow the
criterion above.
